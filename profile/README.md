# IRL Commons

Open standards and open data for IRL streaming: streaming your real life away from your desk, at home, at a venue or on the move.

- **[irl-commons](https://github.com/irl-commons/irl-commons)**: open data. A glossary, a list of apps, services and hardware, links to makers' guides, and the formats and protocols they use. Comes with JSON Schemas and a validator. Data [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (reuse with credit), code MIT.
- **[stream-status](https://github.com/irl-commons/stream-status)** and **[stream-session](https://github.com/irl-commons/stream-session)**: early drafts of open standards. stream-status says what's wrong with a stream, where, and whether it is still live. stream-session lets every device in a show agree on which show it is and who decides right now. No app uses them yet. Comments are welcome in their Discussions.
- **[howtoirl.org](https://howtoirl.org)** is for streamers and their helpers. **[irlcommons.org](https://irlcommons.org)** covers the standards, the data and how to contribute.
- **[OpenStreamBox](https://github.com/openstreambox)** is a reference backpack build that uses these.

## Help improve it

Found a mistake, a dead link or something missing? [Open an issue](https://github.com/irl-commons/irl-commons/issues) or a pull request. No GitHub account? Write to hello@irlcommons.org or ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/KChVwU6fjk).

IRL Commons lists projects without ranking them. Being listed is not a recommendation. IRL Commons belongs to no app or service.
